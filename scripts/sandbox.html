flexmydomain sandbox

Regtest bitcoin, a local relay, and a stand-in for a domain registry's record and DNS. Nothing here reaches a public relay or holds real money. Keys are fresh on every start, for this machine only.

A trade, start to finish

  1. Load the buyer key and the seller key with the links above. On each escrow page click Connect, unlock the stored key with the passphrase.
  2. As the buyer: domain (any name, say lumenary.com), a price like 100000, "I am buying", and the seller's npub. Check the terms, pick the arbiter, and create the invite. Copy the link.
  3. Open that link as the seller with the box above and accept: the reply goes back to the buyer's tab by itself. Both sides save their recovery strings and publish their views.
  4. Fund: copy the escrow address and amount into the faucet below and press Pay and mine 1 block.
  5. As the buyer, sign the refund, and tell the seller where to send the domain: any registrar and account, say Spaceship and buyer@example.com. It lands in the seller's Buyer chat and in their transfer box.
  6. As the seller, pick how you send it, a move or a transfer code, give a payout address from the box below, and press I sent it. The details reach the buyer's Seller chat.
  7. As the buyer, Confirm and pay the seller, mine a block, and the escrow settles.
  8. Then try the other ends: the seller cancelling, the transfer window running out (mine 12), the timelock (mine 144, then recover.html and the broadcast box below), and a dispute: either side asks the arbiter. As the arbiter, open the escrow from the arbiter's list and paste the arbiter key: ask both sides for proof, tick "moved" in the registry box to watch the record change, and rule.
