        How it works

        01

        Rank is the bid

        No algorithm and no editorial picks. The board sorts on sats paid alone, and a tie keeps the earlier payment higher.

        02

        Any domain, no proof

        One registrable name per floor. You do not have to own it and we do not check: paying is the only entitlement this board recognises.

        About

        flexmydomain is a public leaderboard for domain names. Anyone can put any domain on it: there is no proof, no account and no approval. You pay to stand above everyone else.

        Being on this board means somebody paid for it to be here. It is not a claim of ownership and nobody checked one. To say you own a name and put it up for sale, prove it on the market. Listing there is free, and the market checks the proof.

        There is no backend. The ranking is a sum of public Lightning zap receipts held on Nostr relays we do not run; fetch them yourself and you get the same ordering. We keep no record of who paid what.
