How this page works

Everything above came from public infrastructure we do not run: the domains from Nostr relays, the proofs from DNS resolvers. This page is a static file and holds no data of its own. Open your browser's network tab and check.

To prove a domain and add it here, go to the market, where listing is free and the proof is the price. The flex board is the other way round: anyone can put any domain there by paying, and nothing is checked.

A TXT record proves control of the zone. It does not prove you are the registrant, or that you can sell the name: that takes registrar access, and the marketplace checks it separately before any money moves. Prove a domain on the market.
